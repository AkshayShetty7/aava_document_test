## Slide 2

Geography

From the Himalayas in the north to tropical coastlines in the south, India includes mountains, plains, deserts, forests and river systems.

People & languages

India is among the world's most populous countries. Hindi and English are used in many official contexts, alongside a rich range of regional languages.

India’s Constitution came into effect on 26 January 1950, celebrated each year as Republic Day.

## Slide 4

03 • CULTURE & PLACES

Many cultures, one country

Regional identities shape India’s food, festivals, music, clothing and everyday life.

04  /  06

Festivals

Diwali, Eid, Holi, Christmas, Pongal, Bihu, Onam and many local celebrations reflect different faiths and seasonal traditions.

Food & arts

Cuisine varies widely by region. Classical and folk music, dance, cinema, textiles and handicrafts are important parts of cultural life.

Landmarks

The Taj Mahal, Hampi, Jaipur’s historic areas, the Golden Temple, Kerala’s backwaters and Himalayan destinations draw visitors.

A useful way to understand India is to explore it region by region—not as a single, uniform culture.
